Part 1What you get

Four deliverables. Nothing vague.

Each one is a working document your team keeps and uses, not a slide deck that sits in a folder.

1 · Segmentation

Donor segments

Your donors grouped into tiers by giving capacity and engagement, so you know who needs a personal call, who needs a thoughtful email, and who needs to be re-introduced.

2 · Journey

Donor journey map

The path from first gift to renewal to upgrade, plus how to win back lapsed donors. Each stage has a clear next touchpoint.

3 · Cadence

90-day engagement calendar

Which segment hears from you, when, and through which channel. It's built around your team's real capacity, so it survives a busy month.

4 · Session

Live working session

A working session with your team to walk through the plan, assign owners, and make sure it gets used the following Monday.

Part 2The three weeks

How the three weeks go.

  1. Before

    Free scoping call

    Fifteen minutes to confirm the sprint fits. If it does, I send a short agreement and invoice, and we set a kickoff date.

  2. Week 1

    Kickoff and data review

    A short kickoff conversation, then I review your donor data, giving history, and current communications. An export from your database or a spreadsheet is enough.

  3. Week 2

    Segments and journey

    I build your donor segments and the journey map, and share drafts for your feedback.

  4. Week 3

    Cadence and working session

    I finish the 90-day engagement calendar, then we meet as a team to walk through everything and assign owners.

Part 3Is it for you?

Best for organizations ready to stop fundraising in bursts.

A good fit if…

  • You have a donor list, but no clear plan for growing those relationships
  • Most of your fundraising happens around events, deadlines, or emergencies
  • Your team is small and needs a plan it can realistically keep up with
  • You've done the Health Check and want help acting on it

Probably not the right fit if…

  • You don't have any donor records yet
  • You need someone to lead fundraising on an ongoing basis
  • You're looking for grant writing or event planning
Part 4Questions

Common questions.

Do I need to do the Health Check first?

No, but it helps. If you've already done it, we skip straight to building, and your Health Check answers shape the sprint.

What do you need from us?

A donor export (any database or spreadsheet works), a 45-minute kickoff, quick feedback on drafts, and one working session with whoever will run the plan.

What donor database do we need?

Any. I've worked from full CRMs and from a single spreadsheet. The plan is built to work with whatever tools you already use.

How does payment work?

After the scoping call, I send a short agreement and a $1,200 invoice. The sprint starts once both are taken care of.

What happens after the sprint?

You keep everything and run it yourselves. If you'd like to put AI to work on the plan, the AI Fundraising Sprint is a natural next step, but there's no expectation.
