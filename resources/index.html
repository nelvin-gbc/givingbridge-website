FreeResources

Built by Nelvin Johnson · Free to use, always

Two free resources for the people doing this work.

For six years I've worked in nonprofit fundraising, most of it in major gifts, at organizations with budgets from $700,000 to $450 million. Along the way I kept running into the same gaps: no single place to find good trainings, and no clear, honest read on what AI means for our work.

So I built both. They're free, they'll stay free, and they keep me close to what the sector is learning, which makes me a better advisor to the organizations I work with.

Resource 1Events
Tovera

Where changemakers find places to grow, together.

tovera.org

What it is

Tovera is a free, curated calendar of professional events for the nonprofit and social-impact sector across the United States and Canada. That includes conferences, summits, webinars, workshops, networking events, roundtables, and courses. You can search and filter everything in one place, and plan your year instead of chasing it.

Why I built it

As a fundraiser, I was always hunting for the right training for myself or my team, and finding it scattered across dozens of association sites, newsletters, and inboxes. There was no single, trustworthy place to see what the sector was offering. Tovera is that place.

How it stays trustworthy

Every event is confirmed on the organizer's own live page before it's listed, with a real date and a working registration link. A twice-weekly scan surfaces new events from the source list, and a person reviews every public submission. No one can pay for a listing, a ranking, or a feature. Everything is listed on its merits.

Who it's for

Development staff, executive directors, board members, grant writers, and anyone in the sector looking for their next conference, a free webinar, or CFRE credit.

Resource 2AI
The Nonprofit AI Brief

AI is the nonprofit sector's chance to level the playing field.

nonprofitaibrief.org

What it is

A daily guide to what's happening in AI for nonprofits, philanthropy, and social impact. Each pick comes with what it means for your mission and what to do about it, in plain English with a technical layer one click away. You can decide what's worth your time in about five minutes.

Why I built it

I spent years watching the most important work, the relationships and the mission, get crowded out by the repetitive and the manual. When I went deep on AI as a builder, colleagues started asking me the same question: "How does this actually apply to us?" The Brief is my answer, every day.

How it works

Every morning, an editorial system I built scans 51 trusted sources across the nonprofit, philanthropy, nonprofit-tech, and AI-ethics worlds. Each piece is held to a simple standard: is it genuinely about AI and the social sector, and does it have real substance? Most of what's published doesn't make the cut. Summaries help you triage, and every pick links to the original source.

Who it's for

Nonprofit and foundation teams, funders and grantmakers, and consultants and social entrepreneurs who want an honest read on AI without the hype or the doom.
