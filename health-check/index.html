Part 1What I look at

Four areas where fundraising quietly breaks down.

Most organizations don't have a fundraising problem. They have one or two weak links that make everything else harder. The Health Check finds them.

1 · Retention

Are donors coming back?

How many first-time donors give a second time, how quickly you thank people, and what happens between gifts.

2 · Pipeline

Do you know where next year's money comes from?

How concentrated your giving is, whether larger gifts are being cultivated, and how much of the year is spent chasing deadlines.

3 · Board

Is your board's energy going anywhere?

Whether board members know what's expected of them, give and ask, and have a clear role in your fundraising plan.

4 · Rhythm

Is there a steady cadence, or just bursts?

How often donors hear from you, what they hear, and whether your team can keep that pace without burning out.

Part 4Questions

Common questions.

Is this an automated report?

No. I read every questionnaire myself and write the diagnosis for your organization. I use tools to help format the report, but the judgment is mine.

What if I don't have exact numbers?

Estimates are fine. Many organizations don't track retention or gift concentration closely, and noticing that is often part of the diagnosis.

Do I have to get on a call?

No. The Health Check is fully written. If you'd like to talk it through afterward, you can book a free 15-minute call, but there's no obligation.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. Your answers are used only to write your report, and I never share them. After your report arrives, I'll send a few short follow-up emails with practical ideas. You can unsubscribe from those anytime.

What happens after I get my report?

Many organizations can act on it on their own. If you'd like help putting it into practice, the Donor Strategy Sprint picks up exactly where the Health Check leaves off.
