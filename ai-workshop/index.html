Ch. 1What we cover

What we cover in 90 minutes.

Built around the work your team does every week, not AI theory.

  1. 0–15 min

    Where AI helps fundraising, and where it doesn't

    A clear-eyed look at what these tools do well, what they get wrong, and the tasks worth handing over first.

  2. 15–60 min

    Live, on real work

    Together we draft a donor thank-you, prepare a donor research brief before a meeting, and outline a grant narrative, with prompts your team can reuse.

  3. 60–80 min

    Using it safely

    What never goes into an AI tool, how to protect donor data, when a person must review the output, and how to talk about AI use with donors and your board.

  4. 80–90 min

    Your team's questions

    Open Q&A, plus the one use case each person will try this week.

Ch. 3Questions

Common questions.

Does everyone need to be comfortable with AI already?

No. The session works for complete beginners and for people already experimenting. Everyone works on real tasks at their own pace.

Can you tailor it to our organization?

Yes. Before the session, I ask a few questions about your team and the work you do most, and build the examples around them.

Do you offer this for associations and conferences?

Yes. The workshop works well as a session for AFP chapters, nonprofit associations, and conferences. Email me with your event details.

What do we keep afterward?

A prompt pack for common fundraising tasks and a safe-use checklist for your team. You're welcome to record the session for staff who can't attend.

What if we want more than a workshop?

The AI Fundraising Sprint goes further: a workflow audit, a prompt library in your voice, a written AI policy, and team training, in three weeks.
