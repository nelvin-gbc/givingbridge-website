Part 4Questions

Common questions.

Which AI tools do you work with?

The ones your team already has or can get easily, such as ChatGPT, Claude, Microsoft Copilot, or Google Gemini. You don't need to buy new software for the sprint.

Is it safe to use AI with donor information?

It can be, with guardrails. Your policy spells out what never goes into an AI tool, which tools are approved, and where a person always reviews the output. Protecting donor trust comes first.

Do we need technical skills?

No. If your team can write an email, they can use what we build. The training is hands-on and built around your real work.

How is this different from your free AI Playbook?

The Playbook is the do-it-yourself version: templates and prompts anyone can adapt. The sprint is done with you. I tailor the prompts to your voice, fit the policy to your organization, and train your team.

How does payment work?

After we confirm the sprint is a fit, I send a short agreement and an invoice for $1,800. The sprint starts once both are taken care of.
